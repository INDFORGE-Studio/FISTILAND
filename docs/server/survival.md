# Survival and enemies

Survival on FISTILAND is easier than on a stock server, so you spend your time on the real opponents: other players.

## Zombies

Zombies are easy to kill and do less damage than on a stock server.

## Armed NPCs

Armed NPCs are present and easy. They are meant to be a manageable threat, not a wall.

## No mechs

There are no mechs on the map.

## Animals

Plenty of game to hunt.

## Nights

Nights are short and brighter than on a stock server.

!!! note
    Zombie and NPC density is being tested. Feedback is welcome on the Discord.
