# Survival and enemies

Survival on FISTILAND is easier than on a stock server, so you spend your time on the real opponents: other players.

## Zombies

Zombies are numerous but easy: they are weaker, slower and do less damage than on a stock server.

## Armed NPCs

Armed NPCs are present at medium difficulty. They are fragile (one well-placed bullet is enough) and slower than stock. They are meant to be a manageable threat, not a wall.

## No mechs

There are no mechs on the map.

## Animals

Plenty of game to hunt, with more goats, rabbits, donkeys and deer than on a stock server.

## Vehicles

There are fewer vehicles on the map: 10 of most models, 15 Cruisers and 40 bicycles. Getting around takes effort, and engine batteries last much longer.

## Nights

Nights are short. We are tuning them to be brighter than on a stock server.

## Inactive characters

Characters that have not logged in for 30 days are deleted.

!!! note
    Zombie and NPC density is being tested. Feedback is welcome on the Discord.
