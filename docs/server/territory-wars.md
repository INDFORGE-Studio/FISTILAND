# Territory wars

FISTILAND is a base-versus-base server. The ground you hold is what you fight for.

## Flags and turrets

- Each player can place **3 flags**.
- Each base can have **2 turrets**.

## Cargo drops

Cargo drops fall regularly, not constantly. They are a reason to leave your base and a reason to meet other squads.

## Access cards

Up to **6 Killbox** and **6 Apex** access cards can be in play at the same time.

## Fast respawns

Respawns are fast and cheap, so a lost fight costs you time less than a whole evening.

!!! note
    Exact timers and prices are still being tuned and are not listed here.
