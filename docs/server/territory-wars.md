# Territory wars

FISTILAND is a base-versus-base server. The ground you hold is what you fight for.

## Flags and bases

- Each player can place **3 flags**.
- A flag area holds up to **200 base elements**.
- Each base can have **2 turrets**.
- Taking an enemy flag takes **24 hours**.
- Base elements decay twice as slowly as on a stock server, so repairs do not become a chore.

## Raid protection

When everyone from a base is offline, the base is protected for up to **8 hours**. The protection starts 30 minutes after the last player logs out.

New players are protected when they start.

## Cargo drops

Cargo drops fall regularly, roughly every 45 to 60 minutes. They are a reason to leave your base and a reason to meet other squads.

## Access cards

Up to **6 Killbox** and **6 Apex** access cards can be in play at the same time.

## Fame and respawns

- Killing a player gives you half of the fame points the victim loses.
- Respawns are fast and cheap, so a lost fight costs you time less than a whole evening.
- Respawning at your shelter is possible **30 seconds** after your first death.

## Rules

- **Aircraft** are for getting around only. Raiding with an aircraft is not allowed.
- **Mines** are for defending your base only.
- **Third-person view** is disabled.

The first two are rules, not server settings: they rely on players respecting them.
