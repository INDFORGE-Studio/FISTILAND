# Glossary

<p>The words of FISTILAND, in plain language. Every definition comes from the server's own settings.</p>

</div>

## :material-sort-alphabetical-ascending: A to Z

Abandoned bunker
:   A bunker that opens by itself for a while, then closes again. Up to 3 are open at once, each for 12 hours (3 hours when opened with a card). See [Bunkers](server/bunkers.md).

Access card
:   A card that opens a Killbox or Apex facility. 6 cards of each kind exist.

Apex Facility
:   A facility opened with an Apex access card. With the Killbox, it holds the best loot on the map.

Armed NPC
:   A computer-controlled fighter with a weapon. On FISTILAND: medium difficulty, very little health and slower than a player. See [Enemies](server/enemies.md).

BCU
:   The unit that drops when a player is killed. BCU terminals can be locked, and the ones inside abandoned bunkers have a 24-hour cooldown.

Cargo drop
:   A supply crate that falls every 45 to 60 minutes. It is announced 9 minutes before it falls, and it explodes after 20 minutes if nobody opens it. See [Events](server/events.md).

Cooldown group
:   A group of rare items that share one cooldown, which limits item farming.

Coma
:   The state a player can fall into before dying.

Decay
:   The slow wear of base elements that are not maintained. On FISTILAND it takes 56 days.

Dropship
:   An aircraft that brings enemies, such as mechs. Its encounters are switched off, apart from a negligible chance at cargo drops.

Fame points
:   Points earned by playing. A killer takes 50% of the victim's fame points, and any other death costs 10%. Some respawn prices are paid in fame points.

Flag
:   It marks your territory. Each player can place 3, each flag holds 300 base elements, and an enemy needs 24 hours to capture it. See [Territory](server/territory-wars.md).

Gold
:   A currency. Prices ending in g, such as 2g, are paid in gold.

Killbox
:   A facility opened with a Killbox access card. With the Apex Facility, it holds the best loot on the map.

Loot x5
:   Five times the usual chance that loose items spawn and that examined containers give an item. See [Economy](server/economy.md).

Mech
:   A mechanical sentry. There are none on FISTILAND.

Offline protection
:   The kind of raid protection used here. A base is protected when everyone from it is offline: 30 minutes after the last player logs out, and for up to 8 hours.

Prisoner
:   The name the settings give to a player character.

Puppet
:   The name the settings give to a zombie.

Sector respawn
:   Respawning in a sector of the map that you choose. It costs 250 and takes 60 seconds. See [Respawn](server/respawn.md).

Shelter
:   One of the places you can respawn at, for 150 inside a flag area and 2g outside one.

Squad
:   Your team. A squad holds up to 10 players.

Tick rate
:   How many times per second the server updates the world: up to 35 with players online, 5 when it is empty.
