## :material-login: 1. Get in

- [ ] Open the server list from **MULTIPLAYER** in SCUM, then type <button type="button" class="fl-ip" data-copy="31.214.207.109:8159">31.214.207.109:8159</button> in the white field at the bottom right. All the steps are in [Join](server/overview.md#join). The server is free to enter.
- [ ] Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/RHkNGk6ZQN): news, changes, and the place to look for a squad.
- [ ] Read the two player rules in [Rules](server/rules.md): no raiding with aircraft, and mines are for defending your base only.

## :material-parachute: 2. Land and gear up

- [ ] You arrive by parachute and it opens by itself. New players are protected at the start.
- [ ] Loot is x5: search buildings and containers. Loot can come back after 60 minutes once nobody is within 50 m. See [Economy](server/economy.md).
- [ ] Visit a trader: prices are attractive, and fuel and water cost half at stations. Accept a quest: a trader refills its quests every 3 hours.

## :material-weather-night: 3. Survive the night

- [ ] In game, the sun rises at 05:00 and sets at 22:30. A whole in-game day lasts about 6 h 15 min of real time, so a night lasts about 1 h 42 min. Nights stay dark: bring a light. See [Survival](server/survival.md).
- [ ] Vehicles are fewer than usual, so getting around takes effort. Hunt rabbits, deer and goats: there is plenty of game.

## :material-skull-crossbones: 4. Know your enemies

- [ ] Zombies are numerous but slow and fragile. Armed NPCs have medium difficulty and very little health: a well-placed bullet is enough. See [Enemies](server/enemies.md).
- [ ] The real danger is other players. Damage between players is x1.3 and there is no crosshair. There are no mechs.

## :material-flag: 5. Build and hold ground

- [ ] Place up to 3 flags. Each flag holds 300 base elements, plus 17 for every squad member after the first. Try the [base size calculator](server/territory-wars.md#calculator).
- [ ] Capturing an enemy flag takes 24 hours. When your whole squad is offline, your base is protected after 30 minutes, for up to 8 hours. See [Territory](server/territory-wars.md).
- [ ] Squads hold up to 10 players.

## :material-timer-sand: 6. Dying is cheap

- [ ] Know your respawn options, from [Respawn](server/respawn.md):

| Method | Price | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| Random point | 100 | 0 s |
| Your shelter, inside a flag area | 150 | 30 s |
| Chosen sector | 250 | 60 s |
| Next to a squad member | 2g | 60 s |

Prices ending in g are paid in gold; the others are paid in fame points.

## :material-package-variant-closed: 7. Cargo drops and bunkers

- [ ] A cargo drop is announced 9 minutes before it falls, every 45 to 60 minutes. A crate nobody opens explodes after 20 minutes. See [Events](server/events.md).
- [ ] Killbox and Apex bunkers open with access cards: 6 of each exist. Abandoned bunkers open by themselves for 12 hours, 3 at a time. See [Bunkers](server/bunkers.md).
