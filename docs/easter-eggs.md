## :material-magnify: What is an Easter Egg?

An Easter Egg is a hidden surprise left by the developers for those curious enough to find them. It is a long-standing tradition in video games. We left a few on this wiki. They change nothing about the server and give you nothing in game: the only reward is bragging rights and a rank.

## :material-help-circle-outline: The hunt

There are 4 secrets. We will not say where they are, only that the website reacts to the curious.

## :material-trophy: Ranks

Four ranks. Once you earn one, it appears at the centre of the header and stays with you each time you come back. The cards below light up as you progress.

## :material-information-outline: Good to know

- Your progress is saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent to the server. Clearing your site data, or switching device, starts you over.
- Double-click (or double-tap) your rank at the top of the page to reset everything and start again from zero.
- Everything works the same on a phone and on a PC.
- Tip: join the Discord and try to convince a member to explain how to get them quickly.
