## :material-magnify: What is an Easter Egg?

An Easter Egg is a hidden surprise left by the developers for the people curious enough to find it. It is a long-standing tradition in video games. We left a few on this wiki. They change nothing about the server and give you nothing in game: the only reward is bragging rights and a rank.
