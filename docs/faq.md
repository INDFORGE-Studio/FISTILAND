## :material-login: Joining

??? question "How do I join the server?"

    Start SCUM, open the server list from **MULTIPLAYER**, search for `FISTILAND` or type `31.214.207.109:8159` in the white field at the bottom right, then click **CONNECT**. The IP works much better than the search. The steps are in [Join](server/overview.md#join).

??? question "Is it free? Are there paid packs?"

    The server is free to enter. There are no pay-to-win packs, and the Deluxe Survival Pack is disabled.

??? question "How many players can be on the server?"

    14 players at once. A squad holds up to 10 players.

## :material-sword-cross: Playing

??? question "Is it PvP or PvE?"

    Full PvP. The real opponents are other players, while zombies and armed NPCs are easy to handle. New players are protected at the start.

??? question "Are there mechs or dropships?"

    No mechs. Dropship encounters at bunkers and while building a base are off, and the one at cargo drops is practically never chosen. See [Events](server/events.md).

??? question "Can I play in first or third person?"

    Both views are allowed. There is no crosshair.

??? question "How long are the nights?"

    The sun rises at 05:00 and sets at 22:30 in game. A whole in-game day lasts about 6 h 15 min of real time, so a night lasts about 1 h 42 min. Nights stay dark, so bring a light.

??? question "Is my character deleted if I stop playing?"

    No. Inactive characters are kept and never deleted.

??? question "Can I change my name?"

    Yes. A name change costs 500 and can be done once every 168 hours (7 days).

## :material-flag: Bases and raids

??? question "How many flags can I place, and how big can my base be?"

    Up to 3 flags per player. Each flag holds 300 base elements, plus 17 for every squad member after the first, and expansion kits add up to 75 more. Use the [base size calculator](server/territory-wars.md#calculator).

??? question "Is my base safe while I am offline?"

    Yes, partly. When everyone from a base is offline, the base is protected after 30 minutes, for up to 8 hours. Two things stay in the rules: no raiding with aircraft, and mines are for defending your base only.

??? question "How long does it take to capture a flag?"

    24 hours.

??? question "Do bases decay?"

    Yes, if they are not maintained, but slowly: the reference time of 672 hours is stretched to 1344 hours (56 days).

## :material-timer-sand: Respawn, loot and events

??? question "How much does a respawn cost?"

    Random point 100, your shelter inside a flag area 150, a chosen sector 250. A respawn next to a squad member, or at a shelter outside a flag area, costs 2g. Prices ending in g are paid in gold, the others in fame points. See [Respawn](server/respawn.md).

??? question "How often does loot come back?"

    Loot is x5. A world spawner can roll for new loot after 60 minutes when no player is within 50 m.

??? question "When is the next cargo drop?"

    One happens every 45 to 60 minutes. The game announces it 9 minutes before it falls, and a crate nobody opens explodes after 20 minutes.

??? question "How do I get a Killbox or Apex access card?"

    The cards are found in the world: up to 6 Killbox and 6 Apex cards exist, with 3 of each in police stations and 1 of each in the radiation zone. They open the best loot on the map. See [Bunkers](server/bunkers.md).
